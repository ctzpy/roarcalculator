Other miners' deposit held fixed. Marker shows your current position.

Assumptions: you cover all 25 squares evenly (guaranteed presence on the winning square). Settlement rewards split proportionally to deposit share on the winning square, after the admin fee and a treasury fee on the losing pool. Token reward = base round reward + expected jackpot value (pool ÷ 500, the fixed on-chain odds denominator), split the same way.

Mine vs buy compares your effective mining cost per ROAR — k · T · ethPrice ÷ R, where k is the fee drag, T the total board (ETH), R the tokens emitted per round — against the live market price. Mining is cheaper while the total board sits below the break-even pool; beyond it, buying on GigaDEX wins. k is the solo-coverage floor (~10.5%); real settlement loss is worse when others share the winning square, so treat the mining edge as an upper bound. This is expected value across many rounds, not a single-round guarantee, and not financial advice.
